Strategic finance simulation · MBA programmes

Students learn finance by running the company.

Each module puts students in charge of a fictional business through five years of decisions. They explore every concept on a working model, explain it in their own words, and hand in a PDF that drops straight into Canvas. The grades come from replaying their decisions, so a result can’t be faked.

One module, start to finish

Explore, explain, decide, reflect, check

  1. BriefingA board memo sets the situation: here, a discounter lands in the company’s best regions.
  2. 8 conceptsEach concept comes with a task on the model and a written explanation of at least 40 words.
  3. 5 decisionsOne a year, each with a written reason. The simulation plays every year forward.
  4. DebriefOutcome and judgement grades, three possible futures, the effect of each decision, and a reflection.
  5. Check3 multiple-choice questions with instant feedback, and 4 written answers with model answers.
For students

Understanding they have to put into words

  • Concepts learned on a working model, not from a slide: elasticity, margin, operating leverage, runway, and across the six modules NPV, LTV/CAC, ROIC versus WACC and more.
  • Every concept, decision and check question needs a written answer with a minimum word count, about 900 words per module.
  • Decisions are graded twice. Outcome asks how this world turned out. Judgement asks how good the choices were across 12 possible worlds, which separates skill from luck.
  • Immediate feedback on multiple-choice questions, and model answers once the check is submitted.
For lecturers

Assessment you can trust

  • One PDF per student: decisions with reasons, every written answer, check results and grades. It uploads to Canvas like any other assignment.
  • A verification code ties the Canvas PDF to the lecturer’s copy, and any edit changes the code.
  • Grades are never sent by the student. They are recomputed by replaying the student’s decisions.
  • Answers containing pasted text are flagged. The lecturer view lists the whole cohort, with word counts, flags and grades, and exports to CSV.
